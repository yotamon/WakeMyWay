# Brand platform

## Brand name

# Wake My Way

Short mark / monogram:

# WMW

The name communicates both category and differentiation:

- **Wake** anchors the product directly in waking/alarm behavior.
- **My Way** communicates personalization without using "AI", "smart" or technical jargon.

Brand thesis:

> **There is no one right way to wake up. There is your way.**

## Primary messaging

**Primary tagline:**

> **Wake up your way.**

**Product explanation:**

> **An alarm that learns what works for you.**

Strong supporting lines:

- Your alarm knows when. Wake My Way learns how.
- More than a ring. A way to wake.
- Made for the way you wake.
- Your morning, understood.

## Brand essence

Wake My Way should feel:

- Personal
- Calm
- Intelligent
- Witty
- Assured
- Adaptive

Suggested archetype mix:

- Sage: pattern recognition, intelligence, learning
- Caregiver: support without control
- Trickster: a small amount of humor and novelty, mostly expressed through characters

## Tone

The brand itself is not a character. WMW is the system in which characters exist.

```text
Wake My Way
├── Alfred
├── Sam
└── Chaos
```

Brand voice is:

- concise
- specific
- emotionally intelligent
- confident
- calm
- lightly witty
- never hype-heavy

## Copy rules

Do:

- assume user competence
- name the real action
- acknowledge difficult mornings without drama
- use exact facts the user supplied
- keep morning language short

Avoid:

- guilt
- motivational clichés
- fake positivity
- medical/psychological diagnosis
- infantilizing terms
- "AI-powered journey" language
- technical explanations during the alarm session

Examples:

### Ready

> **Tomorrow is ready.**

### Reliability

> **08:00 means 08:00.**

This is an internal brand aspiration / readiness message, not a legal or technical claim that Android can deliver through Force Stop, power-off, uninstall, or revoked system capabilities. External reliability copy must respect the documented platform envelope.

### Offline

> **You're covered offline.**

### Difficult morning

> **That one took a little longer. Tomorrow we'll start movement earlier.**

### Completion

> **Up at 08:04.**

No confetti is required.

## WMW monogram

`WMW` is a secondary brand mark, not a replacement for the full name during early category building.

Potential visual logic:

- interlocking W-M-W geometry
- single-line waveform
- upward/rising central M
- horizon/wave hybrid
- symmetry reflecting adaptation around the individual user

The monogram must remain legible at app-icon sizes and should not rely on fine strokes.

## Logo direction

Avoid literal:

- alarm clock
- bell
- microphone
- robot
- sunrise icon used generically

Preferred direction:

- WMW monogram
- abstract wake curve
- horizon + waveform hybrid
- darkness-to-light form
