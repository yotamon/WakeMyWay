# WakeMyWay Terms of Use — release draft

**Status:** commercial-release draft; legal review recommended before paid public launch  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-24

These terms are written to match the current WakeMyWay product boundary and avoid unsupported claims. Final developer/legal entity, jurisdiction/contact and subscription terms must be inserted before publication.

## 1. WakeMyWay

WakeMyWay is an Android alarm application designed to help a user begin moving at an intended wake time through local alarm delivery and optional behavioral assistance.

WakeMyWay is not medical care, a medical device, sleep-disorder treatment or a guarantee that a person will wake, remain awake, avoid harm or meet an obligation.

## 2. User responsibility and backup planning

Mobile devices, operating systems, batteries, volume/audio routes, permissions and hardware can fail.

Users remain responsible for choosing appropriate backup arrangements when missing a wake could create serious personal, safety, travel, employment, caregiving or financial consequences.

WakeMyWay should not be the sole safety system for emergencies or situations where alarm failure could cause injury or other serious harm.

## 3. Local alarm behavior

WakeMyWay is designed so committed critical alarm delivery, bundled alarm audio, Stop and Snooze do not require a paid entitlement, account or cloud AI service.

Android permissions and device settings can affect exact scheduling, notification/full-screen presentation, microphone use and related functionality. WakeMyWay may show readiness warnings when required capabilities are unavailable.

## 4. Voice and adaptive behavior

Optional voice/motion/learning behavior is intended as assistance, not proof of consciousness or health status.

WakeMyWay may make bounded future adjustments from recorded product behavior and optional user calibration. It does not promise that these adjustments will work for every person or morning.

## 5. Subscriptions

This section becomes effective only when WakeMyWay Pro is publicly offered.

Before purchase, the app/Google Play offer must disclose:
- what Pro provides;
- price;
- billing period;
- whether renewal is automatic;
- trial/introductory terms, if any.

Google Play handles the purchase and subscription relationship for Play-distributed subscriptions. Users must have an easy route from WakeMyWay to Google Play subscription management/cancellation.

Cancellation ordinarily stops future renewal while access continues through the paid period, subject to Google Play terms and applicable local law. Refund rights can vary by country.

No subscription state may prevent a previously committed base alarm from ringing.

## 6. Availability and changes

WakeMyWay may change, simplify or discontinue non-critical features as the product develops. Critical reliability changes are treated separately from optional cloud/AI availability.

We may pause rollout or disable non-critical remote functionality when needed for safety, privacy, security or operational reasons.

## 7. Acceptable use

Do not:
- attempt to bypass platform security or abuse WakeMyWay services;
- use cloud/debug endpoints without authorization;
- interfere with other users or service operation;
- rely on developer-only/internal tools as consumer functionality.

## 8. Privacy

WakeMyWay's handling of user/device data is described in the published WakeMyWay Privacy Policy. The public policy and Google Play Data Safety declaration must match the shipping build.

## 9. Disclaimers and liability

Final liability language must be reviewed for the legal entity and launch jurisdictions. It must not waive rights that cannot legally be waived.

The release must not present WakeMyWay as guaranteeing wakefulness, safety or a medical outcome.
