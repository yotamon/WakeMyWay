# Future opportunities - intentionally outside initial build

These ideas were part of product exploration and are preserved here so they are not forgotten, but they are **not V1 commitments**.

## Zero-screen morning

Potential positioning:

> **Start your day without looking at your phone.**

WMW could summarize what actually matters by voice before the user falls into WhatsApp/social feeds.

Context could eventually include:

- calendar
- important reminders/tasks
- commute
- sleep/wearable context
- selected important email

Guardrail: do not become a generic morning assistant. Context exists to improve activation/orientation.

## Trusted-person wake messages

A friend/partner could leave a short message for a specific morning.

Example:

> "Adam left you something for this morning."

Then play a short recording/message.

Potential viral loop:

```text
Send "Wake me tomorrow" link
   -> trusted person records message
   -> WMW integrates it into Wake Plan
```

Requires careful abuse/privacy controls.

## Shareable Wake Report

A light, humorous summary could create organic sharing:

```text
Today's Wake Report
Alarm: 08:00
Actually up: 08:06
Excuses attempted: 3
Character: Alfred
"Eventually complied."
```

Guardrail: opt-in only and never expose private Tomorrow Contract/calendar information.

## More characters

Potential character archetypes:

- Sam - warm friend
- Alfred - dry butler
- Chaos - playful unpredictability
- Coach - direct physical activation
- Mission Control - day as mission
- Zen - gentle gradual awakening

Do not launch dozens of shallow prompt presets. Each character should have real behavioral rendering, escalation style and boundaries.

## Wearables

Potential uses:

- haptic pre-wake
- richer movement signal
- sleep context

Not required to prove core WMW outcome.

## Smart home

Potential later adapters:

- lights
- speakers
- blinds

These can become strategy actions, but must remain optional.

## Advanced missions

If motion alone is insufficient for some users:

- QR/NFC in bathroom/kitchen
- optional photo mission
- walking

Only add where data proves minimum-friction strategies are insufficient.

## Sleep context

Sleep-stage/sleep-duration context may later inform strategy, but WMW should avoid turning into a sleep tracker unless it materially improves wake outcome.

## Hardware

Long-term, the concept could become a bedside object combining:

- light
- sound
- conversational voice
- physical sensors

The brand and product architecture should not depend on a phone forever, but no hardware work belongs in current roadmap.
